The map shows EFFIS satellite-mapped perimeters from MODIS and Sentinel-2. MODIS generally detects fires around 30 ha or larger; smaller fires may be missed, and perimeters may combine events. Dates are mapping/update dates, not exact ignition or extinction dates. Address comparisons use different data: fire-service municipality totals from 2014–2018. Use the map's Layers control to toggle the EFFIS municipality choropleth and burned-area perimeters independently.

Satellite-mapped fire history

Select a year to show its satellite-mapped EFFIS burned-area perimeters and national annual estimates. The final slider stop combines 2006–2026 and shows all perimeters. Cumulative annual fire counts are sums, not unique-fire counts. Perimeters and annual estimates are separate measures; perimeters do not represent every fire, and 2026 is partial.
